## Recommendations

1. **Ship F3, U2 simple.** Nine of ten gave it 4 or 5, and nobody ranked it below the others. Asked by: all. Do this first.
2. **Do not ship F1.** All ten said it looks like a panda or robot face at 32 px, and several called it toy-like for a tool that handles sensitive interviews. Asked by: Dev, Jonas, Lucia, Mara, Okafor, Priya, Sam, Theo, Walt.
3. **Keep the amber bar.** Lucia noticed it as a hint of unsure words. Hana notes that colour alone carries it, so keep the dark bar next to it so the shape differs.
4. **Keep it clean.** Seven personas said screws and fine lines turn to noise at small sizes. F3 has none.
5. **Not worth doing:** more detail, such as screws or ruling lines (see 4), and the speech-bubble wildcard, which the designer review said reads as a face first.

**Check against the code.** The comparison sheets show 16 and 32 px with the small drawing, which has no screws, so the "screws turn to noise at 16 px" comments are partly about the 48 px and larger drawing. The real exports do the same: 16, 24 and 32 px use the small drawing, so the screws never show below 48 px. The cut still helps at 48 px and in the Dock.

## Notes

- The personas judged pictures, not the running app. Taste in icons is the weakest thing to simulate; no persona represents a designer.
- Okafor and Lucia said the icon does not change what they need. Their scores are about trust, not use.
- Walt (older laptop, reads from a distance) gave F3 the only 5, because nothing tiny needs squinting at.
- No feature was scored above 3 by only one persona, except Walt's 4 for F2.
