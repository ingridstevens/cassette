# Two looks: Fairytale sunset or Series A (mockup)

Today the app has a four-stop slider from "fairytale sunset" to "boring corporate" (plus light/dark). The proposal is to
replace it with **two looks** and a light/dark choice. **Every UI element is identical in both looks: same layout, shapes,
corner radii, fonts, spacing and components. Only the colours change.** Mockup: open `docs/mockups/two-looks.html`, or see the
screenshots `docs/mockups/{sunset,series-a}-{light,dark}.png` (transcript screen with the speakers panel and player).

## F1 Fairytale sunset (kept as it is today)
Peach, rose and lilac gradient background, a gradient pink-to-lilac primary button, rose / amber / violet speaker colours.
Light and dark.

## F2 Series A, the VC-pitch palette (new; name is a placeholder)
Colours only. Light: flat off-white page, white cards, black primary button, indigo / black / green speaker colours.
Dark: near-black page, one acid-lime accent for the primary button, current-speaker dot, waveform and progress bars, with
white and periwinkle as the other speaker colours. No gradients in the background.

## F3 Only two looks, no slider
Remove the four-stop slider, "Remove all fun", the joke Executive options, and the in-between looks (cloudy Tuesday,
open-plan office, boring corporate). Settings shows two large preview tiles, Fairytale sunset and Series A, plus Light /
Dark / Match my computer. Everything works identically in both, only colours differ.
