# App icon: three finalists

Pictures: `icon-sheets/sheet-<id>.png`. Each shows the icon at 256, 48, 32 and 16 px on a light taskbar (top) and 32, 16 and 48 px on a dark taskbar (bottom). All three are flat, petrol (deep blue-green) tile, no gradient, no glow. Amber is the app's colour for "unsure words".
Judge only how the icon looks and feels as the app's icon on your computer: Does it look like a tool you can trust? Does it look like a cassette? Is it friendly or childish? Can you find it in a row of icons?

## F1 U2 (sheet-U2.png)
A white cassette with a pale label that has a dark bar and an amber bar, two big reel holes with white dots looking slightly sideways, and a small notch at the bottom. Friendly. At 32 px it can look like a face (panda or robot).

## F2 U2 better (sheet-U2better.png)
Like F1, but the reels sit in a dark tape window, and there is a pale bottom plate with two holes, four corner screws and a thin line under the label bars. Clearly a cassette, more detail.

## F3 U2 simple (sheet-U2simple.png)
F2 with less detail: tape window with two reels and a plain pale bottom plate, label with a dark bar and an amber bar. No screws, no ruling line, no plate holes.
