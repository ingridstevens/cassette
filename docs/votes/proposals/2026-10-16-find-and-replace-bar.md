# Find and replace bar (as it ships today)

## F1 Find box, match navigation and Replace in the transcript toolbar
The toolbar above the transcript has a find box (`⌘F` / `Ctrl+F`), a match count, previous/next arrows and a **Replace** button.

- Matches are highlighted as you type. Capital letters are ignored. There is no fuzzy matching, no whole-word option and no regular expressions.
- With no match, the box shows the text "No matches" and nothing else (screenshot: the search "deutsche" in a German meeting transcript shows "No matches", while the transcript visibly contains German words with other endings and misheard words).
- **Replace** opens a bar: **Replace in this line** (Enter) or **Replace all**. Changed lines are marked **Edited** with **Put back what the model heard**, and exports use the changed text.
- The bar sits next to **Review N unsure words** (here "Review 150 unsure words") and **Export**. The match arrows are small round buttons.
- Interface text exists in English, German, French and Spanish.
