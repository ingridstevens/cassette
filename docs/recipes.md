# Recipes

## Interview to a Word document
1. Transcribe, then rename the speakers (click a name).
2. Fix mistakes (double-click a line). Mark chapters if you want headings.
3. Export, **Word document**.

## Podcast to chapters, subtitles and show notes
1. Transcribe and add a **Chapter** at each topic start.
2. Export **Chapters (text)** and paste it into your show notes or video description.
3. Export **Subtitles (.srt)** or **Web subtitles (.vtt)**. Use the shift option if your edit is offset.
4. For DaVinci Resolve, export **Markers (.edl)**.

## Checking a transcript before you quote it
1. Transcribe, then press **Review N unsure words** in the toolbar.
2. Listen to each word in its sentence, fix it or confirm it, and press `Enter`.
3. Then read the lines you will quote against the audio: review only covers what the model was unsure of.
4. Export **Original vs. edited** if you need a record of what you changed.

## A conversation in several languages
1. Leave the language on auto-detect and Mixed languages on Automatic. Set the number of speakers if you know it.
2. Use a bigger model than *small* if you can; check the lines tagged with other languages.
3. Fix a wrong language per line (click its language tag) and use **Transcribe this line again in…**; use **Split** where one line holds
   several people.
4. Leave *English translation* on to read the gist under each line, and export Word or text: the translation, the language tags and
   `[overlap]` marks come with it.

## Meeting to minutes
1. Transcribe and name the speakers.
2. **Mark** each decision or action item.
3. Optionally press **Write a summary** in the AI tab, on the Summary sub-tab, and read it through.
4. Export **Meeting minutes (Word)**. Check the summary and the "Decisions and action items" list.

## Soundbites for a podcast or social media
1. Transcribe, fix mistakes, name the speakers.
2. Press **Suggest highlights** in the AI tab, on the Highlights sub-tab, or select the passages yourself. **Keep** the good ones, **Dismiss** the rest.
3. **Save audio clips…** gives you one audio file per highlight. **Export, Highlights (Word)** gives you the quotes with times for captions.

## Research to an anonymised transcript
1. Transcribe, fix mistakes, then export **Original vs. edited** to keep an audit trail of your changes.
2. Export **Anonymised text**. Speakers become P01, P02… and names you typed are replaced. Spoken names of other
   people are not removed: read it before sharing.
