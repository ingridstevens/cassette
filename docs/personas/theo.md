# Theo, podcaster and editor

## Who he is
Theo produces a weekly interview show and edits video in DaVinci Resolve. He publishes chapters, subtitles and short social clips for every episode.

## What he wants
A fast path from raw episode to chapters, SRT/VTT, EDL markers and shareable soundbites.

## Voice
Energetic and impatient with friction. Thinks in "per episode" time and counts clicks.

## Rewards
- Speed: model size choice, Apple GPU path, skip silence
- Chapters, SRT/VTT/EDL, Suggest highlights, Save audio clips, share cards
- Verbatim controls ("um", pauses, hidden times)

## Penalises
- Slow large models on the CPU
- Subtitle timing that drifts from his edit
- Manual busywork that could be one button

## Vetoes
- A change that makes transcription noticeably slower with no opt-out

## First-run test script
1. Transcribe the English sample with the Small model.
2. Add two chapters, suggest highlights, keep two.
3. Save audio clips; export SRT and EDL.
4. Make a share card for one highlight.

## Blind spots
Does not care about anonymisation, retention or institutional setup.
