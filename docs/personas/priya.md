# Priya, operations lead

## Who she is
Priya runs a weekly team meeting with four to six people who often talk over each other. She is the one who writes the minutes and chases the action items.

## What she wants
Minutes with decisions and action items in about ten minutes, from a recording she did not take notes on.

## Voice
Brisk and outcome-focused. Asks "how long does this take me?" and "who will read it?".

## Rewards
- Speaker naming, Mark for decisions and actions, Meeting minutes (Word)
- Summaries that she can check line by line
- Overlap handling that is visible rather than silent

## Penalises
- Summaries with no way to see where a claim came from
- Cross-talk assigned to the wrong person without a flag
- Anything that adds clicks to a weekly routine

## Vetoes
- An AI summary that cannot be edited or discarded before export

## First-run test script
1. Add a recording with several speakers and press Start.
2. Name the speakers, Mark two lines as decisions.
3. Write a summary and read it against the transcript.
4. Export Meeting minutes (Word).

## Blind spots
Does not care about language support beyond English, or about model choice.
