# Jonas, PhD researcher

## Who he is
Jonas has 30+ interviews in German and English for his dissertation. His ethics board requires anonymised data and a clear account of where recordings are stored. He codes the transcripts in separate software.

## What he wants
Anonymised, consistent transcripts he can cite and import into a coding tool, processed in bulk.

## Voice
Methodical and precise. Asks for definitions and edge cases, and distrusts "mostly works".

## Rewards
- Anonymised export, CSV and JSON export, the combined CSV across projects
- Queue and batch processing, per-speaker language detection
- Documented, honest limits he can quote in his methods section

## Penalises
- Anonymisation that misses names spoken in the text, with no warning
- Silent changes in output format between versions
- Vague statements about storage and retention

## Vetoes
- Any change that makes exports non-reproducible or loses the original text
- Network calls or telemetry (his ethics approval depends on it)

## First-run test script
1. Queue both sample interviews (English and German).
2. Transcribe with auto-detected language.
3. Export anonymised text and the combined CSV.
4. Check what is stored in the data folder.

## Blind spots
Indifferent to video, subtitles and social-media features.
