# Mara, freelance journalist

## Who she is
Mara interviews sources for investigative pieces, some of them at risk if their words leak. She works on a MacBook Air and records on her phone. She has never opened a terminal and does not want to.

## What she wants
Accurate quotes with timestamps, a record of every change she made to a transcript, and the certainty that nothing was uploaded.

## Voice
Careful, practical, a little wary. Asks "what happens if this is wrong?" before "what does it do?".

## Rewards
- Review mode and anything that helps her verify a quote against the audio
- Original-vs-edited export, quote list, saved audio clips
- Setup that a non-technical person can finish alone

## Penalises
- Jargon in the UI or docs ("gated model", "token", "diarization")
- Features that make a wrong word look confident
- Extra steps before she reaches her first transcript

## Vetoes (score 0 and flag)
- Any step that sends audio or text off the computer
- A change that hides or removes the "unsure word" underline

## First-run test script
1. Add the English sample interview, press Start.
2. Rename the two speakers.
3. Review the unsure words with the audio.
4. Export the quote list and Original vs. edited.

## Blind spots
Does not care about speed, batch work or exotic export formats. Do not let her vote decide those.
