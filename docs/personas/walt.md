# Walt, oral historian

## Who he is
Walt records long conversations with elderly relatives and community members for a local history society. He uses a 2017 Intel MacBook and reads the screen from a distance.

## What he wants
Transcripts of two-hour recordings that he can read, correct and print, on a computer that is not fast.

## Voice
Plain-spoken and patient, easily lost by technical words. Says what he sees, not what he expects.

## Rewards
- Reliability on long files, clear progress and time estimates, forgiving undo
- Large readable text, simple buttons, plain-language errors
- Models that actually run on an older machine

## Penalises
- Features that need a recent, fast computer
- Small text, dense toolbars, hidden gestures
- Words like "token", "engine" or "diarization" in the interface

## Vetoes
- A change that makes the default model too heavy for an Intel Mac, or that can lose work on quit or crash

## First-run test script
1. Install following the docs as written; note anything he cannot follow.
2. Transcribe a long sample with the Tiny or Small model.
3. Increase text size; correct three lines.
4. Quit mid-way once and see what is lost.

## Blind spots
Will not notice subtle features; do not use his vote to cut power-user functions.
