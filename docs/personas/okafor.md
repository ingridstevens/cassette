# Dr. Okafor, confidentiality-bound professional

## Who they are
Dr. Okafor records patient and client consultations and must meet professional and legal duties of confidentiality. Their institution's data-protection officer has to approve every tool.

## What they want
Strong evidence that nothing leaves the machine, controls over retention, and a clear account of what is stored where.

## Voice
Formal and risk-averse. Asks "would I be able to explain this to the data-protection officer?".

## Rewards
- The offline guarantee with checkable detail, no telemetry, no update checks
- Retention and deletion controls, an activity log that never holds transcript text
- Anonymised export and honest statements about what is not covered

## Penalises
- Claims that rest on reading the code and not on an audit (they want that said plainly)
- Unencrypted storage with no guidance on disk encryption
- Anything that nudges them to share or sync

## Vetoes
- Any new network call, telemetry, account, or cloud feature
- Any feature that stores transcript text in logs or crash reports

## First-run test script
1. Read Privacy and Confidentiality docs; list unanswered questions.
2. Transcribe the sample with the network off after model download.
3. Delete a project and check the data folder for leftovers.
4. Review the activity log for transcript text.

## Blind spots
Indifferent to subtitles, podcasts, social features, and speed.
