# Chapters

**A chapter marks where a new topic starts in a long recording, with a title you choose.** They help you and your
audience find their way around, and you can export them as a ready-made list.

## When they're useful

- **Podcasts and videos:** paste the list into your YouTube description or podcast show notes, and listeners
  can jump to a topic.
- **Long interviews and meetings:** a table of contents for yourself ("12:40 Budget discussion").
- **Editing:** export the same points as timeline markers for DaVinci Resolve (see [Exporting](exporting.md)).

## How to use them

1. Hover the line where a new topic begins and click **Chapter**.
2. Type a title (it suggests the first words of the line) and **Save**. The line shows a chapter flag with the title.
3. To change or remove it, click **Chapter** on that line again.
4. Export: **Export → Chapters (text)**. The menu item is greyed out until you have marked at least one chapter.

## What the file looks like

```
0:00 Intro
2:15 Why audio archives matter
9:40 Naming files properly
1:02:30 Wrapping up
```

- One line per chapter: the time and the title. Hours appear only when needed.
- YouTube requires the first chapter to be at `0:00`. If your first chapter starts later, the file adds
  `0:00 Intro` for you.
- Chapters are saved with the project.
